Coverage and explicit ROI selection

Observed-span integration is partial volume. Whole-organ coverage adds explicitly confirmed zero-area endpoints; these remain reconstruction assumptions.

A unique TOTAL ROI is preferred. Local fragments are never silently treated as a complete layer.

Evaluate against independent annotations

A reference annotation is a comparison, not error-free truth. The same animal must not cross training and test splits.

Preparing a paired comparison

Each pair needs the same original-image hash, prediction and reference ROI sets, explicit layer coverage, calibration and model training provenance. Unknown or unassessed layers remain missing. Structure evidence and applications

Use observed contours for section matching and spatial plots. Export the inferred surface to a 3D viewer with µm units; it is not a measured tissue volume. Keep the reconstruction JSON for source hashes and assumptions.

Atlas reference and rigid landmark alignment

Supply a licensed atlas mesh in µm. An atlas is a separate reference; registration never replaces native specimen coordinates or measured volume. A PDF alone does not establish a complete individual anatomy.

Compare animals, not section counts

Only compatible, reviewed native-coordinate measurements are compared. Small cohorts support descriptive results.
