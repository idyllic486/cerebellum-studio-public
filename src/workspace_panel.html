You decide what is accepted.

The program handles file checks, calculations and visualisation. Researchers verify tissue boundaries, calibration, anatomical identity and whether results are suitable for their study.

Automated ROI generation is experimental and requires a validated compatible model. It is not required for manual review. Keep your original files and export your reviewed work.

The path your research follows here.

Three stages, in this order. The small label on each stage names the tab in the menu above where it happens.

  1. 1

    In Image analysis

    Open a section and outline its layers.

    Open one Nissl section image. Its layer outlines can come from ImageJ ROIs you already have, from a draft by the built-in model, or from your own drawing.

    Download a synthetic example section A drawing with its ROI file, for trying the steps. It is not tissue.

    You can
    Import an ROI file, let the model draw a draft, or draw by hand. Correct any outline in the browser, or in ImageJ or Fiji and bring it back.
    You get
    Layer outlines on your own image, ready for you to check.
  2. 2

    In Image analysis

    Review and approve.

    Compare every outline with the tissue, confirm the pixel size, and approve the section yourself. A model draft never approves itself.

    You can
    Show the image, the outlines, or both. Move, relabel or remove an outline, then approve the section.
    You get
    Approved ROIs, a measurement table and a review record to download.
  3. 3

    In Study & 3D

    Build a study and see it in 3D.

    Bring the approved sections of several animals together, see them as a stack in 3D and compare groups.

    You can
    Add reviewed sections to animals, enter position, thickness and group, then align and compare.
    You get
    A 3D stack for each animal, comparisons between groups, and a study file you can reopen.
