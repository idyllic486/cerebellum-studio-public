Program and researcher

Generated boundaries are drafts. Internal evaluation does not establish anatomical accuracy, and model output never approves itself.

Program: Source checks, model fitting, draft generation and measurements. Researcher: Biological identity, uncertain boundaries, calibration and final acceptance.
