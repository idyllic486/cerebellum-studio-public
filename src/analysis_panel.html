    Interpretation guide

    Open your TIFF, bring existing ImageJ boundaries or draw your own, then review and approve measurements. Automated drafts require a compatible trained model; none is supplied as a validated Nissl model.

    Original files stay on this computer. Clearing an image also clears its dependent review from the workspace after saving a recovery copy. Original files are never deleted.

    Use trained models to draft boundaries on a new TIFF. Existing ROI import and manual review remain available below.

    Choose ROI ZIP or .roi files drawn on this original TIFF, up to 16 MiB total. Areas, open lines and points retain their distinct geometry. Unknown labels remain unassigned until reviewed.

    The local runner accepts an externally supplied ONNX segmentation model and its manifest. Its output is a draft, not an anatomical diagnosis or a validated measurement. Download the workflow guide (English) · Download the local runner. Python 3.12 and external trained ONNX weights are required.

    Single-page uncompressed 8-bit gray or RGB strip TIFFs support up to 256 MiB and 80 million pixels. Other supported TIFFs are limited to 128 MiB per file, 40 million pixels and 128 MiB of decoded channels. Files are decoded locally in a background worker; only the display preview is reduced. Only a single section is reviewed here. A stitched image of that section is supported; an image containing several different sections must be separated first. Physical area requires measured X and Y scales. Depth and thickness are retained as metadata; this tool does not infer volume from one section.

    A readable TIFF can still use an unsupported container. Prepare a compatible copy locally; selected-page pixels and original hashes are checked. This does not establish model compatibility. TIFF compatibility guide (English) · Download TIFF preparation tools.

    Coordinates refer to the original TIFF, even when its preview is reduced. The preview displays 8-bit colour; low-range 16-bit images may look dark. Original pixel values are retained for inference and export. Polygon interiors and holes determine geometric area. ROI coordinates use ImageJ’s float32 precision. Geometric screening flags layer overlap and boundaries outside CB. Screening is not anatomical validation; review any flags and record your decision. Review layer boundaries; CB may overlap its constituent layers, and their areas must not be added to CB. Absence of a class remains missing, not zero. Approval records a researcher’s review; it does not establish model accuracy.

    Create an approximate boundary draft from the native prediction maps. Block voting changes pixels; the original maps and the exact change counts are retained. Review the new boundaries before approval.

    Drag the image to pan. Select an ROI, then drag a nearby vertex or edit its coordinates. Dashed boundaries are excluded.

    View changes hide or show layers only. Original pixels, ROI coordinates, measurements and approval are unchanged.

    Boundary review

    Draw a polygon by clicking its boundary, then finish. At least three vertices are required.

    Correct or redraw this ROI

    Redrawing replaces all contours of the selected ROI. Nested contours become holes; separated contours remain components. Original boundaries remain in the evidence and history.

    Compare an existing measurement CSV

    Optional source context, up to 2 MiB. Original cells and empty values are preserved. Exact, unambiguous ROI names may be linked; comparison requires an explicitly named unit column. Imported values never replace reviewed geometry or calibration. Compared values are shown as source / reviewed / difference; agreement does not validate a shared boundary definition.

    Section identity and physical coordinates

    Geometry screening

    Flags identify relationships to inspect, not anatomical errors. Fissures, tears and missing tissue are never filled automatically. CB and its layers must not be summed.

      Separate ROI files

      About these files

      The ROI scope controls the visible boundaries and separate file downloads. Excluded ROIs retain their CSV row with empty measurements and are omitted from ROI.zip.

      Separate files retain source and approval identifiers in the CSV. Download the approved results archive for the original files and full review history.

      Before approval, the draft can be saved as ImageJ ROI files, corrected in ImageJ or Fiji, and imported again under ROI files. A draft file is not an approved result and carries no measurements.

      The file holds Cb, GL+WM and WM as polygons, one per separate part or hole, so their vertices can be dragged in ImageJ. ML and GL are worked out again when the corrected file is imported.

      Browser recovery keeps the image, review and pending fields when saving succeeds. Download a separate backup: the approved ZIP includes ImageJ ROIs, measurement.csv, the review record and unchanged source files.

      A draft contains the prediction and review history. Reopen the matching source TIFF first. Imported approvals are retained as history; a fresh review is required.
