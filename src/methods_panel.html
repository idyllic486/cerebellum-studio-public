Comparison methods and scope of interpretation of results

What is being compared?

The reference method interpolates, between sections, an SDF representing distances inside and outside tissue boundaries. The comparison method interpolates the SDF after matching centroids and axis-wise scale. Neither method restores nonlinear deformation or tissue damage.

How does hold-out validation work?

The shape of the observed plane under evaluation is not used for prediction or for defining the computation grid. Predictions are made from the remaining observed planes and compared with the held-out plane. Scores may change if the data range or grid resolution changes.

What are disagreement and sensitivity?

Inter-method disagreement is conditional on these two methods. The ±1 pixel test, which expands or shrinks boundaries by one grid cell, measures sensitivity to boundary changes and is not a statistical confidence interval.

How far can the candidate ranking be trusted?

An exploratory ranking based on the spacing between observed planes and the disagreement between the two methods. The benefit of actually observing the next section requires separate prospective validation. The 20 hold-out validation scores for the example and the results of this experiment are stored separately.
