Source and interpretation

The atlas source is shown when the reference has loaded.

Outlines are placed in the coordinates of the annotation volume. The same scale applies to all three axes. Lines have no anatomical thickness.

The series runs from the midplane toward one side. The annotation is symmetric, so the other side is its mirror image.

The selected plane is amber. Other planes are grey-blue. These are outlines of an average-brain annotation: the whole cerebellum, the granular layer with white matter, and the white matter with the deep nuclei. The Purkinje layer is not drawn. They are not observed tissue or approved segmentations.

Placed sections are human-drawn ROI outlines put at the position of the most similar atlas plane. The match is an estimate, left and right are not distinguished, and nothing here is a measured reconstruction or approved anatomy.

The optional surface is interpolated between published filled-compartment contours. It is a review model, not the complete cerebellum, a tissue measurement or a specimen reconstruction.
