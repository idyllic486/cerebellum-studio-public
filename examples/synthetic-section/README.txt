Synthetic example section for Cerebellum Studio

synthetic-section.tif        a drawn picture, 8-bit RGB. It is NOT tissue and NOT a microscope image.
synthetic-section_ROIs.zip   ImageJ ROI file with Cb, ML_TOTAL, GL_TOTAL, GL+WM and WM for that picture.

Pixel size: 5 micrometres per pixel (the picture is far coarser than a real montage).

How it was made: one sagittal plane (1012.5 micrometres from the midplane) of atlas outlines was painted in flat
colours with a coarse texture. The ROI file holds the outlines the picture was painted from.

Use it to try opening an image, importing ROIs, reviewing and exporting. Do not use it to judge a model or to
measure anything: a model trained on real stained tissue is not expected to draw sensible layers on a flat drawing.

Outlines: Piluso S, Veraszto C, Carey H, Delattre E, L'Yvonnet T, Colnot E, Romani A, Bjaalie JG, Keller D. An extended and improved CCFv3 annotation and Nissl atlas of the entire mouse brain. Imaging Neuroscience 2025. doi:10.1162/imag_a_00565. Built on the Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate Framework: Wang Q et al. Cell 2020;181:936-953.
