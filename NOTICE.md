# What the licence covers

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the source code of Cerebellum Studio written for this project: the site under `src/`, the tools under `scripts/`, the checks under `qa/` and the build script.

It does not cover the following, each of which keeps its own terms.

| Material | Terms |
|---|---|
| Libraries under `vendor/` and Three.js | Their own licences, kept beside each file |
| Atlas outlines under `assets/atlas-open/` | CC BY 4.0 as stated by their source, built on the Allen CCFv3 with its own terms; see the notice in that folder. Not MIT |
| Drawings under `src/atlas/` derived from a printed atlas | Not cleared for redistribution. No build includes them, and a public repository must leave them out |
| Research images, ROI files, measurements and anything derived from a laboratory's animals | Not licensed here. A public build leaves them out |
| Trained model weights under `assets/models/` | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Credit: "Cerebellum Studio tissue-layer model (2026), Kim Min Seong". They are data, not code, and are not under MIT |

This file describes the project's intent. It is not legal advice, and it does not grant rights that belong to someone else.
